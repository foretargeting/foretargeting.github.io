How it works

Our intention simulator runs in your browser, ten times a second. It sees both hands, the object's shape and its last few poses, and predicts where the object goes next. Nothing is physically simulated.

The solid hands trail your keys by 0.2 s, because the model also reads where the hands are about to go. The faint hands are where yours are now.

The model never saw the held-out objects in training. Scans come from our Aria recordings; the rest are simulation meshes.
